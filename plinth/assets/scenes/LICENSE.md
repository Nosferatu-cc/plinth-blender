# Scene maps

Scanned texture maps used by Plinth's scenes. They are not covered by
Plinth's GPL licence:

- Poly Haven textures (https://polyhaven.com): CC0 1.0, public domain.
- ambientCG leaf atlases (https://ambientcg.com): CC0 1.0, public domain.
- Earth: NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble Next Generation (Reto Stöckli,
  NASA) and Black Marble 2016 (NASA Earth Observatory / NOAA). NASA imagery
  is not copyrighted (public domain); credit NASA Earth Observatory.

`index.json` lists every set, its source page and its real size in metres.
Rebuild with `tools/build_scene_maps.py`.
